PDFMango is a free, open-source PDF tool that works entirely in your browser. Merge, reorder, rotate and delete pages, turn photos into PDF pages, and shrink big files to email size.

Your files never leave your device: no upload, no sign-up, no ads, on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone.

“Every PDF tool I tried was full of ads, cluttered, or wanted my documents on its server, so I had one built that is none of those.” — Venkatarangan
