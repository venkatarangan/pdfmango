# PDFMango 0.4.0: follow-up requests

Asked by Venkatarangan Thirumalai while 0.4.0 was being built, after the main request
([2026-11-v0.4-straighten-and-split.md](2026-11-v0.4-straighten-and-split.md)). All are built in 0.4.0.

## Fixes asked for

- **Phones at 320 px:** the app bar and the About page scrolled sideways, and the More menu ran off the screen.
- **Page preview:** double-clicking a page showed it zoomed in, with part of the page cut off; it must fit the whole page. Hovering the close button made the view shake.
- **A full test of the app** on desktop and phones, and fixes for what it found: the download preview at 320 px, the menu hidden under the message bar, the Adjust split title, and the Split wording about Scan compression.

## Straighten and Split on real photos

Straighten and Split book spreads found nothing on the owner's own phone photos of a newspaper and an open booklet
(kept out of the repository). They were made to work better on photos like these:

- Split finds a fold that shows only as a thin line or a step in brightness, and a wide page with a fair guess is split and flagged **Check split** instead of being left alone.
- Straighten accepts small real tilts (about 1°), and both tools look at a page the way it shows after it is rotated.
- When nothing happens, the message says why and what to try instead.

## New tools

- **Crop:** mark an area of a page and keep only that, "useful especially now that we have deskew". For one page or all selected pages.
- **Suggested corners in Straighten photo:** the four corners are placed on the sheet's edges automatically (no OpenCV).
- **Auto in Crop:** finds the paper on a darker background, or trims a clean page to its text.
- **A straightened split half turns about its own middle**, so it no longer slides and loses a strip at its edge.
